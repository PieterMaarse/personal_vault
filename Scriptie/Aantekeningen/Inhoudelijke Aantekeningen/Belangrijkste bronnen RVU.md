• **"Kamerbrief uitwerking akkoord 'Gezond naar het pensioen'" (28 May 2025):** This is the definitive policy document describing the shift from a temporary "relaxation" to a **structural RVU exemption** starting in 2026. It details the three-year evaluation cycle and the political agreement between the government and social partners. [[Kamerbrief uitwerking akkoord _Gezond naar het pensioen_.pdf]]

• **"Handreiking voor uitvoering RVU’s vanaf 2026" (20 May 2025):** This provides the practical framework for the new structural phase. It is essential for citing the new **monthly payment preference**, the mitigation of bankruptcy risks for employees, and the explicit link between RVU usage and investments in **sustainable employability**. [[Handreiking uitvoering RVU.pdf]]
